## D0b findings (2026-11-13; hand estimates only, no circuit files; PLAN.md section D0b has the detail and the "Choices I made" table)
- ARCHITECTURE: N = 8 x (amplifier + 12-bit SAR), no mux, simultaneous sampling at CS falling, 4 kS/s per channel, one DOUT behind a digital serializer (the 8 SAR result registers chained into one 96-bit shift register, reused, +12 mux2 per ADC), config on DIN.
- FRAME: 160 SCLK = 10 x 16-bit words. SCLK 1-16 (word 0): DIN = {7 x 0, MODE, EN[7:0]}; SCLK 1-19: the 8 conversions, staggered one SCLK per channel (ADC i does trial k - i at SCLK k); SCLK 33-160: 8 words {0, ID[2:0], code[11:0]}, channel 0 first, same-frame data (no latency).
  Config hold register loads at SCLK 33 (after all conversions; frames with < 33 clocks change nothing); dbs is clocked by SCLK (no CLK pad); DOUT driven only while CS is low (pad _OE = ~CS). Pins: CS, SCLK, DIN, DOUT, RSTN (no new pad vs the 31-pad list).
- CLOCK: SCLK 0.64 MHz minimum (frame = 250 us), 1-5 MHz recommended; T_ACQ = 250 us - 19/f_SCLK = 220-246 us because each ADC reconnects to its amplifier right after its own last trial; CS from an MCU hardware timer, jitter <= 85 ns rms (0.1 LSB at 300 Hz full scale).
- ACQUISITION: no reset to 1.65 V (removed). At reconnect the amplifier output has moved <= 3.6-17.9 mV (5-1 MHz) from the held value; SIMULATED A4b no-reset case (+-0.24 V, 245 us) <= 0.26 LSB is therefore a conservative bound; follower sink stays BM_KSF 6.
  Power-up / large step: ESTIMATE 4-22 mV at 240 us for a 1.65 V step, discard ~10 frames.
- SUPPLY: MSB trial draws 13.55 pC (not 27 pC), 36.1 pC per conversion; 8 simultaneous ADCs draw 108 pC in one SCLK cycle, staggered 35.9 pC (3.0x less). Decap for a 50 mV peak: 2.17 nF (0.47 mm2) vs 0.72 nF (0.16 mm2) (MOS density 4.6 fF/um2 ESTIMATE, unchecked).
  Ring-down with L = 2 nH (ASSUMED) needs series R >= ~0.25 ohm; at 0.1 ohm nothing settles. First-order supply charge per trial is data-independent (repeats every frame).
- AREA / POWER: 0.136 mm2 for 8 ADCs + shared digital (decap excluded); ~28 uW (1.4 % of the amplifiers). Error budget RSS 0.44 LSB (two new crosstalk lines).

## D1 findings (2026-11-14; comparator only; DAC per user update 2026-11-13: split 6+6, 5x5 um MIM units, C_s = 7 pF; all numbers SIMULATED from sim/d1_*.log unless marked ESTIMATE; summary sim/d1_summary.txt)
- CELL (spice/comparator.spice cell `cmp_p`, sizes spice/comparator_params.spice; xschem/cmp_p.sch + cmp_inv.sch): PMOS-input double-tail dynamic comparator, evaluate = clk high. Pair 80u/0.5u, tail 4u/0.28u, reset NMOS 2u, 40 fF MIM/MOS cap on each preamp node,
  latch control 8u/0.5u, latch 4u/0.28u. `cmp_n` (NMOS-input dual) is spice-only, kept for the common-mode contrast. Input capacitance per input 70 fF (reset) / 190 fF (evaluate) = 1.0-2.7 % of 7 pF (attenuation of the comparator input, a fixed gain error of the trip voltage only).
- TIMING / ENERGY (sim/d1_delay.log; 30 ns cycle): decision time at 0.1 mV overdrive 1.5 / 1.3 / 1.8 ns (typ 27 / ff 85 / ss -40), 0.7-1.1 ns at large overdrive (CM 1.65 V); energy per decision 6.0 / 7.0 / 5.8 pJ (about 8-9 pJ at CM 0.6 V), supply charge about 1.8-2.6 pC per decision.
  Per conversion 12 decisions = 70-110 pJ per ADC, comparable with the DAC energy (119 pJ, D0b): ADC power estimate rises by about 3-4 uW for 8 ADCs at 4 kS/s (ESTIMATE; still < 1 % of the amplifiers). Sampling the comparator at SCLK <= 5 MHz leaves > 90 ns margin.
- OFFSET (Monte Carlo sw_stat_mismatch=1, 40 seeds x 3 corners, sim/d1_offset.log): sigma 1.27 / 1.31 / 1.23 mV = 1.57 / 1.63 / 1.53 LSB (typ / ff / ss), max |offset| 3.3 mV. Meets the D0 criterion (<= 2 LSB). Pair-only mismatch is about 1.3 mV, the second stage adds about 0.9 mV (control 8u/0.5u was chosen over 4u/0.28u: 1.78 -> 1.55 mV in 12 seeds, sim/d1_sizing.log).
  FIXED OFFSET IS A PURE CODE SHIFT for the conventional scheme (see the V* result below), so sigma 1.6 LSB only moves the code; it is not nonlinearity.
- NOISE (injected thermal noise on the input pair, gamma = 1, probit fit of 600 decisions, sim/d1_noise.log): input-referred 118 / 168 / 108 uV rms (typ / ff 85 / ss -40) = 0.15 / 0.21 / 0.13 LSB against the 0.25 LSB comparator allocation: margin at ff 85 C is 0.04 LSB (16 %).
  LATCH NOISE NOT MEASURED (OPEN, for D4's ENOB run): two latch-noise injection methods were tried and rejected (constant-gm parallel source: unphysical, 646 uV; series gate noise sources: .tran aborts, three corners, sim/d1_sizing.log); the final 600-cycle runs aborted at all 3 corners. Rough ESTIMATE only: gate-referred latch noise / preamp gain (5.7-8.3 at the Mc turn-on instant) of order 50 uV at ff 85 C, NOT a result.
  System level (why it cannot change a decision): comparator noise is divided by the channel gain of 1000, so total input-referred noise stays sqrt(1.847^2 + 0.168^2) = 1.855 uV at ff 85 C (typ 1.672, ss 1.544), and even the rejected 646 uV figure would give 1.957 uV; both < 2 uV. DAC kT/C at 7 pF = 24.3 uV = 0.030 LSB (D0 line was 0.020 LSB at 16.4 pF).
- COMMON-MODE RANGE (sim/d1_icmr.log; symmetric +-d/2, correct at +-1 LSB and trip point within 0.5 LSB of the 1.65 V trip, which is flat at 50 uV over the whole window): PMOS-input 0..2.5 / 0..2.7 / 0..2.2 V (typ / ff / ss), delay grows to 6-15 ns at the upper edge; NMOS-input dual 0.7..3.3 / 0.5..3.3 / 0.9..3.3 V.
  DEMAND (scripts/d1_icmr_need.py, sim/d1_icmr_need.log), CONVENTIONAL scheme: 1.24-2.06 V over the amplifier range (Vx; |diff| <= 0.825 V), 0.83-2.48 V over the full 0-3.3 V range; only CM = 1.65 V is accuracy-critical (|diff| <= 4 LSB there). Measured window covers the amplifier-range demand at all 3 corners; the full-range demand is met at typ 27 and ff 85, and missed at ss -40 (2.2 V < 2.48 V)
  but that does not matter: the fixed-reference test (Vref = 1.65 V, Vx = 0..3.3 V, sim/d1_conv.log) gave the correct sign at ALL corners for both cells, and small-difference decisions occur only near 1.65 V, inside 0-2.2 V.
- MONOTONIC SWITCHING is NOT chosen here; recorded as a D2 option. Demand (pseudo-differential, exact enumeration): CM 0.58-1.90 V over the amplifier range (0-2.48 V full range), accuracy-critical CM spans 0.58-1.65 V (0-1.65 V full range), i.e. the common mode MOVES with each trial, so a CM-dependent offset becomes nonlinearity (INL), not a fixed shift. The PMOS-input cell covers the range (its trip is flat to 0.4 mV over 0..2.2 V at all corners), the NMOS cell does not (starts at 0.5-0.9 V).
  Costs: about 2x DAC capacitance (each side 4096 u: about 14 pF with the 7 pF DAC), a reference-side array; benefit: far less supply charge per trial (D0b open item), the charge is drawn at sampling instead of at decisions.
- KICKBACK (direct check on a FLOATING 7 pF top plate, pre-charged to 1.65 V +-0.4 mV, other input an ideal 1.65 V source, 12 decisions at 1 and 5 MHz, 3 corners, sim/d1_float.log): NET drift after each decision and after 12 decisions <= 0.0001 mV (< 0.001 LSB; limit 0.25 LSB): PASS, nothing is left on the plate after the reset.
  BUT DURING THE EVALUATE PHASE the plate is pushed up by +14.8 / +19.4 / +9.8 mV at the decision instant (+18 / +24 / +12 LSB, typ / ff / ss; peak 22 / 26 / 19 mV), because the input-pair gate charge (about 100-150 fC) is pulled out of the 7 pF plate as the tail node rises; it returns during the reset. With a +-0.4 mV start every -0.4 mV decision comes out wrong, so the shift is a fixed offset of the decision threshold.
  (An earlier version integrated charge from ideal sources and counted the testbench stepping the inputs to the next value; its 'Qp +24.8 fC / Qn -16.5 fC' rows were that artifact. The windowed charge table is not converged (window-length dependent) and is NOT used; sim/d1_kick*_ARTIFACT.log are kept for the record.)
  SHIFT vs Vx and the trip V* (sim/d1_float_vs_x.log, 5 MHz): s(Vx) falls with Vx, typ 22.1 mV at 1.24 V to 6.2 mV at 2.06 V (slope -20 mV/V; ff 26.8..8.7, ss 19.2..3.8 mV), so Vx + s(Vx) rises monotonically (slope 0.94-0.98) and the comparator has ONE trip voltage with the reference at 1.65 V:
  V* = 1.6349 / 1.6303 / 1.6397 V (typ / ff / ss) = a pure code shift of +18.7 / +24.4 / +12.7 LSB, plus the 1.5-1.6 LSB mismatch sigma. A fixed shift of this size is acceptable because the amplifier output is AC-coupled (the offset is removed by the signal chain; it costs about 0.5 % of the 4096 codes).
- SUPPLY SENSITIVITY of that shift (sim/d1_vddsens.log, VDD 3.25 / 3.30 / 3.35 V): with a reference and plate that TRACK VDD/2: 0.19 / 0.17 / 0.21 LSB per 10 mV (typ / ff / ss); with a FIXED 1.65 V reference: 0.36 / 0.31 / 0.38 LSB per 10 mV. So per-trial supply droop at the decision must stay <= about 10 mV (not the 50 mV assumed in D0b) for 0.2 LSB.
- EQUIVALENCE (sim/d1_equiv_xschem.log): xschem == hand netlist: PASS (structure of cmp_p 20 devices and of cmp_inv; delay, ICMR (ss -40 / ff 85) and kick runs agree within 1.4 %). First attempts showed 100 % differences because the xschem netlist errored (cmp_inv defined twice without default parameters); the Monte Carlo offset is not used for the comparison (equal seeds assign the random draws to devices in netlist order). The noise-injection sources are not in the cell (scripts/mk_noisy_cell.py writes a testbench-only copy).
- Not done: latch noise (above); dependence of the evaluate-phase shift on the ACTUAL DAC (bottom-plate switching, parasitics: the 7 pF is an ideal cap to ground); fs/sf corners; flicker noise of the comparator (not relevant at 12 decisions per 250 us, state); CS/clock-jitter effects; layout parasitics; the sampling switch.
- Fix options for the evaluate-phase shift (D2/D4, NOT implemented; the shift is only a code shift today, so none is required): (1) a matched 7 pF cap on the reference side (a pseudo-differential top plate), so the kick appears on both inputs and cancels: about +7 pF of MIM per ADC (about +3,500 um2, ESTIMATE at 2 fF/um2, +0.03 mm2 for 8); (2) a fully differential DAC (2x the capacitance, also the monotonic option); (3) a smaller input pair (shift scales about with W, 80u -> 40u halves it) at a noise penalty of about sqrt(2) in the input-referred noise (168 -> about 240 uV at ff 85, over the 0.25 LSB allocation) and 1.4x the offset sigma; (4) more preamp capacitance does not help the shift (it comes from the pair gates), it only lowers noise.
