## 4. Substitutions and things that differ from the prompts / SPEC (all deliberate, all in the generator)
1. **The PDK pcells have off-grid shapes** (DRC `*_OFFGRID`, 5 nm grid): the transistor pcells draw Metal1 gate pads with 1 nm coordinates (y = 2.289 um); `cap_mim` rounds sizes to 2 nm (44.595 -> 44.596) and places its Via4 sea on a
   1 nm grid (about 20,000 off-grid vias in the six big pieces). `amplib.snapped_copy` / `mim_snapped_copy` copy a pcell into a plain cell and grow the Metal1 / Metal2 boxes outward to 5 nm (never shrink), round FuseTop / CAP_MK / MIM_L_MK / Metal4 /
   Metal5 to the nearest 5 nm and shift each Via4 as a whole (its size must stay 0.26 um). Every device then has DRC 0 and the LVS sizes stay inside 1 %.
2. **pcell `bulk='Bulk Tie'` is unusable here**: it coerces W to >= 0.42 um, and in the first Step-1 run Magic put the drain port of the two devices on the well net (cause not investigated; the drawing with our own tie matched at once). The pseudo-resistor transistors (W 0.22) use `con_bet_fin=0` and their own N+ tie (0.42 x 0.5 um comp, DF.9 area 0.2025 um2,
   NP.3a / NP.5di: N+ at least 0.16 um from the P+ comp and 0.16 um beyond the N+ comp). `con_bet_fin=0` is also what gives W = 0.28 for the one-shot's 0.28u/40u transistor (default coerces to 0.36).
3. **A dummy transistor is a device for netgen** (lp_dummy: a transistor with gate = source = drain on one net stays an unmatched device; `nf=2` against `m=2` merges fine, "Merged 1 parallel devices"). Dummies are therefore
   POLY-ONLY lines on field oxide (min(L, 2 um) wide, 0.3 um beyond the comp, at both ends of the matched groups of amp_ch: input pair, tail mirror group, xm3 / xm4 mirror): they are no device in Magic and not in the reference. Diffusion dummies were not drawn.
4. **No `Bulk Tie` / `Guard Ring` pcell options anywhere**: `amplib.ring_cl` draws a P+ (substrate) or N+ ring with its own n-well (centre line on integer um, Metal1 ring 1 um wide, contacts, 0.16 um implant overlap).
5. Labels inside imported library cells (the 7t5v0 OR2 and filltie) would become ports of amp_ch in Magic: `amplib.or2_block` deletes them; pins are labelled exactly once, on the top cell (14 labels = the 14 pins of the reference).
6. Net names inside blocks are NOT labelled (a label becomes a pin); netgen's net-mismatch report names nets by their fan-out, which was enough (the first LVS of the full cell already matched uniquely, only 3 W property errors).

## 5. MIM design rules measured with the deck (DRC variant D), AFE/layout/amp/mk_mimtest.py and flowtest/mimsp_*.gds / mimst_*.gds
- two pieces with unrelated bottom plates: top plates 2.4 um apart are legal (bottom plates 1.2 um apart); 1.8 um is not (MIMTM.1).
- an UNRELATED Metal4 strap between two pieces must be >= 1.8 um from each top plate edge (= 1.2 um from the bottom plate edge): 1.5 um fails, 1.8 um passes.
- Metal4 that is part of a bottom plate polygon (tab, strap, pad) must be >= 1.2 um from the "virtual" region = FuseTop sized by 1.06 um clipped to that polygon, i.e. **>= 2.26 um from the top plate edge**: a pad 2.21 um away fails (MIMTM.1, corner
  case). The corridor between two pieces of one Cin block therefore needs 2.26 + 0.9 (strap) + 1.8 = 4.96 -> drawn 5.5 um.
- Via3 may not touch the bottom plate under FuseTop (MIMTM.10), a Metal4 tab outside the plate with Via4 -> Metal5 pad is legal (tested: tab 3 um, pad 1.2 x 1.2 um). Every piece has a sea of Via4 (0.26 um, 0.5 um spacing) over the top plate.

## 6. Generator structure (AFE/layout/amp/)
- `amplib.py` helpers: `Dev` (pcell instance + terminal rectangles found from the pcell geometry), `dev_block` (multi-finger array: S rail Metal1 below, gate bus Metal1 above with a 1 um landing pad, drains Via1 + Metal2 stubs to ONE Metal2 rail,
  ring), `add_tile` / `group_block` (single-finger devices with three 0.5 um Metal1 landing pads on integer coordinates), `pr_block` (pseudo-resistor pair, own wells), `mim_block` (MIM + tabs + 2 x 2 um Metal4 pads), `or2_block`,
  `compose`, `ring_cl`. **Every landing pad is on an integer um coordinate** so that the router grid (1.0 um pitch) meets it: the origin of every block is an integer.
- `router.py`: 1 um grid router, Metal2 / Metal3 / Metal4, 0.5 um wires and pads, Via1 only at Metal1 terminals; A* from the tree built so far; obstacles with the net they belong to; terminals with few access nodes are reserved; "halo" = soft clearance of
  about 2 um around the long wires of sensitive nets (cost, not a hard block); `hard` = extra clearance that only keeps other nets away (MIMTM.1). Wires of one net near (not overlapping) a fixed shape of the same net are blocked too (spacing).
- `blocks_amp.py`: the circuit as blocks with their nets (flattened net names of the reference); `mk_amp_ch.py`: Cin block (manual Metal5 / Metal4 geometry), rows, column, router, pins, GDS.
- Tool order for the next cell generator (L1a): write the blocks with integer-aligned landing pads, run the router for everything that is not a plate, run DRC, then LVS; the debug aid that saved most time was `AFE/layout/amp/drcsum.py <drc dir>`
  (rule, coordinates) and `shapes_at.py` (what is at x, y).

## 7. L2a rework (ACCEPT.md ladder A-3, 2026-11-20): another packing of the same generator
Same blocks, same netlist, same LVS reference (no `AFE/layout/amp/lvs/`); only the placement section of `mk_amp_ch.py` and three ring-gap knobs of `amplib.py` changed. Runs in 7 s (generator) + 7 s (DRC) + 1 s (LVS).
- **Commands (exactly as run, repository root)**: generator, `python3 AFE/layout/scripts/flow.py drc --gds AFE/layout/amp/amp_ch.gds --top amp_ch --out AFE/layout/amp/flow`, `python3 AFE/layout/scripts/flow.py lvs --gds AFE/layout/amp/amp_ch.gds --top amp_ch --ref AFE/layout/lvs/afe_chc.spice --out AFE/layout/amp/flow`,
  `bash AFE/layout/scripts/kl.sh AFE/layout/amp/check_amp.py gds=AFE/layout/amp/amp_ch.gds build=AFE/layout/amp/amp_ch.build.json outjson=AFE/layout/amp/check_amp.json`, `python3 AFE/layout/scripts/gate.py L2a --gds AFE/layout/amp/amp_ch.gds --run` (output: `AFE/layout/amp/gate_L2a.log`).
- **Knobs** (kl.sh `name=value`, defaults of `lay=2`): `rg gg cg` = ring gap (um) of dev_block / group_block / compose (1.5 / 1.5 / 1.5; the first pass 2.5 / 2.5 / 3.0), `gx gy` = gap between blocks of a row / between rows (1.5 / 1.0), `strip` = height of the Cf1 strip under the Cin block (15),
  `capt` = margin above the Cin lanes (2), `s2p_up` (1), `x0` (7), `xr` (4). A scratch sweep of 14 variants was run (one generation + DRC + LVS each, 20 s); results below.
- **Limits found with the official deck (variant D)**: ring gaps below 1.5 um (rg = gg = cg = 1.0) give M1.2a (Metal1 spacing, ring against the rails) and a pin mismatch in LVS; **Cf1 strip 13 um (MIMTM.1 x4 and an LVS pin mismatch) and 14 um (MIMTM.1 x2) fail**; the cause was not analysed, the likely one is the Cf1 pieces coming within 1.8 um of the Cin bottom plate; 15 um is clean, strip 11 um makes the router fail (vn, vp, vdd, vss);
  gy = 0.5 with gx = 1.0 gives MIMTM.1 x2; gy = 1.0 / gx = 1.5 is clean. A larger sensitive-net halo (`halo=3.0`) costs 1 um of width and does not change the clearance numbers.
- **What the packing does**: three rows of 50 um devices under the Cin block (bottom: sink1 / xms / fol; middle: xm3 / xm4 mirror + xm6 + Cc1; top: the input pair + tail group, directly under the Cin descenders; east of the pair: the gate-mux / park / one-shot / OR2 devices, s2tail, s2p), the Cf1 strip with the two Cf1 pieces and pseudo-R 1 / 2 between the pair and the Cin block,
  one column east of the Cin block (s2pair, Cf2 + pseudo-R 3, Cin2, Cc2, C_T) so that all stage-2 nets are short (vn2 124 um of wire instead of 550). vdd / vss pins moved to the top edge at (W - 6, H - 5) and (W - 16, H - 5) (the east column now reaches the old place).
- **25 um instead of 50 um fingers (lever 1 of the rework prompt): NOT used.** The area of a finger array does not shrink with finger width (same total W x L; the height overhead of rails / bus / ring stays, the width overhead grows: ESTIMATE, not drawn: xm6 as 8 x 25 um would be about 143 x 38 um = 5,400 um2 against 73 x 58 = 4,300 um2 now), and a changed finger width changes the extracted
  `w` property against the reference (w = 50 u, m = n): it was not tried with netgen, so whether the 1 % property check would accept it is NOT known.

### 7.1 Step 2 of the rework: tail group merged, pair and xm6 in one row (`lay=3`, default; asked for after the first rework commit f1472dd)
- **Tail-group merge (`tm=1`, implied by `lay=3`)**: the three tail units xmb / xmt / xm7 (2 fingers 50u/4u each, gate ibias1, source vdd, drains ibias1 / tail1 / v1) are ONE 6-finger pfet array (strips S D S D S D S, each drain strip its own net, Metal2 stub to its own pad on an integer x:
  `dev_block(..., multi=True)` in amplib.py; one gate bus, one landing pad, one set of poly dummies, `dummy_groups=[[0,1,2,3],[4]]`). netgen merged the fingers as before ("Circuits match uniquely", same reference). `ota_p` 150.6 -> 142.6 um wide, so `ota_p` + xm6 + 1.5 um gap = 217 um share one row (`xr=1`).
- **Placement `lay=3`**: bottom row = mirror xm3 / xm4 (left) and a 115 um column (xms / fol / Cc1 / s2p on the bottom with the `out` pin, sink1 above); next row = `ota_p` + xm6; then the strip (24 um) under the Cin block with OR2, pseudo-R 1, Cf1, the sp / sn gate-mux stack, Cf1', pseudo-R 2; east column = s2tail, s2pair, Cf2 + pseudo-R 3, Cin2, Cc2, C_T.
  vdd / vss pins moved to the east edge at (W - 4, 14) and (W - 4, 30), Metal4 3 x 3 um.
- **Limits found**: strip 21 um: the router fails (sw_rec_b, sw_rec, t, nd); strip 22 and 23 um are DRC 0 / LVS match but **check_amp.py says `under_cin_clean: false`**: the ring of the sn block reaches the 0.6 um margin of the Cin bottom plates (comp, contact, Metal1, P+ under A1 and B1, 5 to 26 um2):
  DRC and LVS do NOT see this (Decision 46c is a reservation, not a rule of the deck). 24 um is clean. **Always run check_amp.py after a repack, not only DRC / LVS.** Row gap 0.5 um (gy) with strip 22 um: MIMTM.1 x2. Sensitive-net halo 2.5 / 3.0 um: no change of the vn clearance numbers (a hend = 5 variant even failed to route vdd).

### 7.2 Step 3 of the rework: east-column rotation of mir (`lay=4`, tried, NOT adopted; the committed GDS is `lay=3`)
- `amplib.rotate_block(ly, B, name)` rotates a block by 90 degrees (terminals, fixed shapes, bbox transformed; integer pad coordinates stay integer). `lay=4`: mir (xm3 / xm4) stands rotated in the east column (58 x 93 um, west edge at x = 160, i.e. up to 5 um under the Metal5 lanes of the Cin block, where only Metal5 and the Metal4 ref tab run),
  C_T and Cin2 move to the bottom row next to the xms / fol / Cc1 / s2p / sink1 column; s2tail, s2pair, Cf2 + pseudo-R 3, Cc2 stay in the east column. Rotated MOS blocks pass DRC and LVS (no poly-orientation rule in the deck).
- **Result (strip 24 um, the clean value): 218.75 x 305.31 um = 66,788 um2, DRC 0, LVS match, `under_cin_clean: true`: 4 um (1.3 %) less than `lay=3` (309.25 um)**, because the bottom row only drops from 58 to about 55 um (its column of sink1 + xms / fol / Cc1 / s2p is 51 um + gaps) and the east column overflows by 2.7 um (Cc2 ends above the Cin lanes).
  The cost: vn2 wire 290 um (lay=3: 124), o2 479 (337), o1 292 (320), d1 254, vn2 clearance 0.5 um with 2 near nodes (lay=3: 2.5 um, 0), because Cin2 / C_T are now far from s2pair / Cf2. Strip 23 um with `xmr=158`: the router fails (o2) and the Cin plates are not clean.
- Why it cannot give the 20 um still missing: a rotated 50 um block is 58 um wide and the east column (53 um) is full (180 of 186 um); whatever it takes in, a capacitor has to leave, and the bottom row cannot shrink below the 51 um of its sink1 column. The real remaining levers are A-2 (-14 um per row) and A-4 (-23 um once) of ACCEPT.md, or fewer channels (A-6).
