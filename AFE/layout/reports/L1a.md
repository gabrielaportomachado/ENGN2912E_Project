## 1. Summary and verdict
- adc_ch (dac_sw + cmp_p + vhalf + vrefs + inv_1) is generated by `AFE/layout/adc/mk_adc_ch.py` into `AFE/layout/adc/adc_ch.gds`: **DRC 0 violations (official deck, variant D) and LVS "Circuits match uniquely." against AFE/layout/lvs/adc_ch.spice (not regenerated, 34 pins)**; the Cref keep-out passes (nothing below Metal4 in the 59.07 um plate footprint).
- **Gate L1a: FAIL (1 FAIL): the slot. adc_ch is 265.85 x 126.25 um = 33,564 um2 against 220 x 104 = 22,880 um2 (+47 %; the F1 estimate was 14,911 um2).** Ladder A has no entry that helps (section 6). Same situation as amp_ch after L2a (84,466 um2 against 57,860 um2), so the open question is the floorplan:
  eight channels of the two cells as built need 1.049 mm2 of the 1.000 mm2 outline before corridors and rings (section 8). Nothing else fails: L1b (PEX / post-layout simulations) can start on this GDS.
- First phase after L2a: "L2a findings" and the L2a Results-log row were copied unchanged into AFE/NOTES.md and converted into AFE/layout/reports/L2a.md (common rule 7).
- Findings the next phases need first: the DAC pitch of SPEC.md 3.3 (6.8 um) is not legal (7.4 um between different nets), dummy MIM units cannot exist (netgen counts them), see section 7.

## 7. Deviations
1. DAC unit pitch 7.4 um (different nets) / 6.0 um (same net) instead of 6.8 um (MIMTM.1: top plates of different nets >= 2.4 um); array core 104.9 x 72.4 um instead of 122.4 x 68 um; the bottom-plate nets leave through tabs and pads (Metal4 pads >= 2.26 um from the plate, rows 3 / 4 through a 6 um channel) instead of from below.
2. The dummy ring is a plain-metal row (Metal4 6.2 um, Metal5 5 um, vss) south and north only: a dummy MIM unit with both plates on vss is extracted as a shorted cap_mim device and netgen does not drop it (test: 162 vs 128 devices, NO MATCH); the west / east sides carry the exits.
3. Small bits: b0 / bd (and b6 / the bridge) are the row ends of rows 3 and 4 instead of the centre (the bridge has to sit at the boundary of the halves, SPEC.md 3.3 rule 1; the pairs keep the point symmetry). Effect ESTIMATE negligible (0.02 % of a unit per 23 um at 1 %/mm).
4. The reset switches sit next to the lower ends of the t / m trunks and the Cref switch + inverter between the comparator and Cref (SPEC.md puts all switches in the driver row; vref < 30 um needs the Cref switch at Cref; t / m wires stay short).
5. The driver zone is two rows of 11 and 14 um plus routing (29 um) instead of 14 um; the keep-out band of 20 um is measured from the gate wires of the last driver row to the first real plate row.
6. Cell size 265.85 x 126.25 um (slot 220 x 104 um); the pin x positions of SPEC.md 3.3 are kept (control pins x = 12 .. 96 on Metal2 at y = 0, supplies Metal4 at the inner edge), vin on Metal3 at the outer edge x = 4 um, vdd Metal4 at the east edge (not 220 um away from the DAC: the cell is wider than the slot).
7. Supplies are routed like signals (router) in this cell; L3a replaces them by straps. The driver sources are tied to their rings in Metal1.

## 8. Open issues and risks
1. **Area / floorplan (the one FAIL).** adc_ch 33,564 um2 (+47 % over the slot, +125 % over F1), amp_ch 84,466 um2 (+46 % over its slot): 8 channels = 0.269 + 0.676 mm2 + afe_dig slot 0.090 + bias_genc slot 0.014 = **1.049 mm2 against the 1.000 mm2 outline before corridors, rings and the 30 um pin ring**. Options: re-pack adc_ch and amp_ch (levers in the L2a report: 25 um fingers, landing pads, the stage-2 MIM column; for adc_ch: comparator as one
   column below Cref, multi-finger driver rows, vhalf pitch 2.2 um instead of 3 um; the DAC array (about 8.5k um2) and Cref (3.6k um2) cannot shrink: they already match F1) and re-tile the channel (cell width <= 235 um); or N = 4 (ladder A-6) with a re-packed adc_ch; or N = 2 (A-7). A decision for the monitoring session / REVIEW.md before L3a.
2. Not tested by DRC / LVS and left to L1b: parasitics on t / m / vref (wire lengths in section 4; Metal5 trunks cross the Metal4 exit tabs), bit-net resistance (164 - 379 um of 0.5 um Metal2 / Metal3 per bit: about 40 ohm per 220 um, ESTIMATE), the bridge step at 2112 (Q-3), settling at 3 MHz (Q-5), the comparator offset (Cxa units are 5 x 5 um as in the reference).
3. Antenna / density not run (L3a / L4).
4. The comparator crossing nets o1 / o2 are on different tracks: the halves are mirror images, the crossing is not.
5. The 22.18 um vhalf capacitor and the serpentine sit under no sensitive node; the serpentine uses Metal2 lanes over the poly bodies (no DRC rule against it).
