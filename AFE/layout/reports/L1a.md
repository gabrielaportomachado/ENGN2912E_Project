## 7. Deviations
1. **No dummy rows around the DAC array.** The two plain-metal bars (Metal4 6.2 um / Metal5 5 um, net vss) south and north of the 8 rows are not drawn (they gave the L1a build 14.8 um of height; they are not MIM units and have no matching value for the MIM; SPEC.md 3.3 rule 1 asked for a ring of dummy MIM units that cannot exist, FLOW_ADC.md section 3). Effect ESTIMATE: edge units b11 / b5 (rows 0 and 7) see the metal environment of the array edge instead of a bar; no first-order effect on the capacitance ratio.
2. DAC unit pitch 7.4 / 6.0 um, exits through tabs and pads: unchanged from L1a (deviation 1 of the first build).
3. Small bits b0 / bd and b6 / bridge at the row ends: unchanged.
4. **vhalf is folded** into 8 rows (A B A B A B A B) of 62.5 um = 4 segments per resistor of 250 um (L1a: 20 rows of 25 um), pitch 2.2 um, **no dummy poly lines at the ends**, and sits in the keep-out band under the DAC (where SPEC.md 3.3 recommended it); netgen sums the series segments: 2 ppolyf_u_1k of W = 1.0, L = 250 each (match). The 22.18 um capacitor sits in the column east of the DAC.
5. Comparator: pair as 5 fingers of 16u / 0.5u (total 80u, netgen merges them: match), sub-blocks 3 um apart, routing margin 2 um, inner exposure pads at +-8 um; the centre column and mirror structure are unchanged. Driver rings 1.0 um from the tiles (was 2.5 um), pull-down tiles at pitch 4 + 3 um (a tied source needs no pad column).
6. **Pins:** control pins unchanged (x = 12 .. 96, Metal2, south edge); vdd_cmp / vdd_dac / vss pins unchanged (110 .. 130 / 140 .. 160 / 170 .. 210, Metal4, y 0 .. 4); **`vdd` moved from the east edge to the south edge (Metal4, x 215 .. 221, y 0 .. 4)**: the Cref plate fills the east edge (ladder G-5: L3a connects it to the spur corridor with a short wrap-around wire; the spur is at x 222 .. 229 of the cell frame); vin as before (Metal3, x = 4, top edge).
7. **Keep-outs (SPEC.md 3.3 rule 6, section 5 rule 1):** 26 of 28 control nets stay >= 21 um from every top plate; **rst_sw** runs 5.0 um from the t trunk at its reset switch xr0 (and xr1 at the m trunk): 38 nodes (about 20 um) inside 20 um, all at the two devices it drives; **tg_on** passes 14.8 um from the t trunk and 20 um from the Cref plate over about 24 um of wire in the column (Cref switch gate + inverter). Both nets are quasi-static during a conversion (rst_sw and tg_on change only at the sampling instants, while t is held on vcmd by rst_sw): ESTIMATE, to be confirmed by the L1b transients, not simulated here. The router keeps every control net out of the band y 24 .. 44 except 12 um at xr0 / xr1 (`rt.ban`).
8. **Floorplan of the cell:** the cell uses the slot as 219.5 x 103.67 um with the bbox from x = 1.5 to 221.0 (L3a: pin x positions are measured from the cell origin as in SPEC.md 3.3); Cref block 63.3 x 60.3 um with its bottom-plate pad on the WEST side (3 um); the vref node is strapped to the plate on Metal5 from the comparator pad (6.5 um) and from the drains of the Cref switch (west edge of the plate); t strap runs between the comparator top (y 41.6) and the first plate row (Metal5 at y 42.7 .. 43.7, 0.5 um from the plate row: DRC clean).
9. Supplies are routed like signals (router) in this cell; L3a replaces them by straps (as in L1a). The driver sources are tied to their rings in Metal1.

## 8. Open issues and risks
1. **No slack in height (103.67 of 104 um).** R3 (macro 80 um, or 85 / 90 um) and R4 (amp_ch) must not ask adc_ch for height: any extra macro height comes out of amp_h. If R2's `adc_h` is ever raised, nothing in this cell needs it.
2. Not tested by DRC / LVS and left to L1b: parasitics on t / m / vref (t 79 um, m 87 um of Metal5; the vref strap 6.5 um + the switch strap about 30 um; the comparator input pair is 2 .. 5 um from the lowest plate row of the DAC, the comparator's clock wires are inside the cell), bit-net resistance (144 - 213 um of 0.5 um Metal2 / Metal3 per bit: about 40 ohm per 220 um, ESTIMATE), the bridge step at 2112 (Q-3), settling at 3 MHz (Q-5), the comparator offset, the effect of the missing dummy bars on the b11 / b5 edge rows, the coupling of tg_on / rst_sw (deviation 7).
3. Antenna / density not run (L3a / L4).
4. The comparator crossing nets o1 / o2 are on different tracks: the halves are mirror images, the crossing is not.
5. vhalf without end dummies: the A row at the bottom and the B row at the top each have one open side (ESTIMATE: the resistor ratio is what counts; the serpentine alternates A B so that both resistors see the same neighbours except at the two ends).
6. The `vdd` pin is on the south edge at x 215 .. 221 (L3a wrap, deviation 6).
