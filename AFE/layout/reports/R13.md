# R13 report (2026-11-21; Claude Sonnet 5.5): REC pad loading on the amplifier input (Decision 53 risk), simulation only

## 1. Summary and verdict
**Verdict: with the REC pad OPEN (the board rule "high-impedance probe only, or left open") the amplifier is not changed: every one of the 87 limit rows of accept_limits.json (Decision 51) PASSES with the pad, on the extracted amp_ch (87 / 87) and on the schematic (87 / 87), typ / ff 85 / ss -40, design and junction convention. Nothing has to change in any cell, netlist or limit.** The pad (PDK cell gf180mcu_fd_io__asig_5p0 + 2 nH / 0.25 ohm bond wire + 2 pF) adds 2.81 / 2.88 / 2.93 pF (ff 85 / typ / ss -40) and at most 1.9 pA of leakage (ff 85) to the rec node; gain moves <= 0.0003 dB, f_lo <= 0.0006 Hz, f_hi <= 0.03 Hz, noise_d8 <= 0.0001 uV, PSRR <= +0.0014, power-up error <= +0.04 mV (section 4).
**The pad is NOT harmless with a 10 pF / 10 MOhm scope probe on it (DC coupled): 15 of 87 rows FAIL on the extracted amp_ch, 14 on the schematic.** The probe draws 1.64 V / 10 MOhm = 164 nA from rec; in the design convention the output stays put but the reset recovery after a 10 / 100 mV overshoot does not return to 1 mV inside the limit (typ 17.0 s, ss -40 22.0 s against <= 12 s; ss -40 final value 1.823 V against 1.6 .. 1.7 V), and in the junction convention (the risk view) the amplifier loses its operating point (gain 29 dB at typ, -1.9 dB at ss -40; output 0.80 / 0.40 V). What-ifs (item 4, all simulation only): a 1 GOhm probe PASSES all 87 rows; 100 MOhm: 6 FAIL; an AC-coupled probe (100 nF in series) is worse (21 FAIL + 1 WARN); a 10 kOhm / 100 kOhm series resistor between rec and the pad does not help (13 FAIL with 100 kOhm), it only costs area and an extra node. **Board rule that the results support: REC open, or a probe of >= 1 GOhm DC (FET probe); no 10 MOhm scope probe, no AC-coupled probe.** Decision for the team; none is made here.
Two things the next phase must know: (1) the long reset-recovery rows (amp_reset t_1mv_s_os100m / os10m) move by +-2 .. 3 s from perturbations of 1 fF, i.e. they are NOT a measure of the pad (controls, section 4 table E) and their margin to the limit (12 s) is thin with or without the pad; (2) ngspice 46 returns onoise_total = nan as soon as a PDK diode is in the circuit, lptb.py integrates the spectrum itself with --rec-load (validated to 6 digits, AFE/layout/sim/recpad/noise_check.md).
No gate of its own exists for R13 (gate.py has no R13 phase); gate L2b re-run after the lptb.py change: PASS (section 5).

## 8. Open issues and risks
- **Board / test rule (team decision, not made here): REC must be open or probed with >= 1 GOhm DC (FET probe, section 4 G). A 10 MOhm scope probe breaks the reset recovery (and, in the junction risk view, the operating point); an AC-coupled probe is worse; a 100 kOhm series resistor does not fix it.** Candidates for the text of the rule: "REC: high-impedance probe point, >= 1 GOhm DC, <= 10 pF; do not connect a 10 MOhm scope probe or a DC source".
- The reset-recovery rows with the 100 mV overshoot (limit 12 s) swing by +-3 s from perturbations of 1 fF (section 4 H); at typ the result without any pad is 9.47 s, so the pass margin of this row is not a robust number for any later netlist change (L3a bias_genc, supply coupling). Consider judging recovery on the 0 / 1 / 10 mV rows and the host-reset rows, which are stable to 0.1 s.
- The ESD structure of the real chip (pad ring cell, series resistance, guard rings) may leak more than the PDK diode model: 1 nA is inside, 10 nA leaves the junction-convention vout window (what-if D). First-silicon test 4 (host rule 6) should read the idle REC level.
