## 3. MIM array rules measured with the deck (DRC variant D) - they decide the size of the DAC
- unit = cap_mim pcell: top plate 5 x 5 um (FuseTop + Metal5), bottom plate Metal4 = top plate + 0.6 um on every side, CAP_MK = the bottom plate.
- neighbours of DIFFERENT nets: top plates >= 2.4 um apart (bottom plates 1.2 um: MIMTM.1). The 6.8 um pitch of SPEC.md 3.3 (1.8 um) is NOT legal: the minimum pitch is 7.4 um.
- neighbours of the SAME net may be 1.0 um apart (MIMTM.6 >= 0.6 um): the Metal4 plates overlap and are ONE polygon, the Metal5 plates are joined by a 1 um strap (pitch 6.0 um). The array is built from RUNS of one net (rows 0,1,6,7 of a half are
  whole rows of the largest bit, rows 2 and 5 the next one, rows 3 and 4 the small bits).
- a bottom-plate pad (Metal4 polygon that is part of the plate) must be >= 2.26 um from the top plate edge of its own unit (1.06 um virtual region + 1.2 um): pads are 1.2 x 1.2 um, tab 1 um wide; Via4 only on such a pad (never on the plate).
- a dummy MIM with both plates on vss is extracted by Magic as a SHORTED cap_mim device and netgen does not drop it (the reference has none): the dummy rows are plain Metal4 (6.2 um) / Metal5 (5 um) bars of the unit footprint (no FuseTop, no CAP_MK).
- Metal5 (variant D, 11 kA): width 0.44, spacing 0.46, area 0.5625 um2 (a 0.5 um pad is too small: use 0.8 um).
- the big MIM pcells (59.07 um) have a Via4 sea of pitch 0.760 um; `adclib.info_mim` rebuilds it with the exact pitch for w >= 10 um so that the 5 nm snapping cannot create 0.495 um spacings (MIMTM.9 / V4.2a).

## 4. Changes to the L2a helpers (adclib.py)
1. `add_tile`: the straight source / drain stubs ran 0.16 um beside the gate pad of narrow devices (M1.2a, 0.23 um): the stubs now leave the strip sideways (its own y-range), then run up on the pad column. Options `ring_s` / `ring_g`: the source (or the gate of the dummy
   driver) goes straight down / left to the ring of the group in Metal1 (same net): no router terminal, no power routing for the driver rows.
2. `SNAP_LAYERS` also snaps DualGate and V5_XTOR (the 5 V pcells draw them with 1 nm coordinates). Tile kinds `nfet5` / `pfet5` = 5 V pcells (the inverter of the samb signal is drawn from them: netgen flattens inv_1 to nfet_05v0 / pfet_05v0).
3. `info_mim`: regular Via4 sea (see above).
Poly resistor: `ppolyf_u_high_Rs_resistor` with l_res / w_res (NOT r_length), 12.5 um body + heads, with its substrate-tie contact clipped (it would sit under the jumper lanes); series segments of equal width are one resistor for netgen.
netgen: a layout cell that has the NAME of a subckt of the reference (my comparator block was called cmp_p) is compared as a separate circuit and fails pin matching: the block is called cmp_blk, so that netgen flattens both.

## 5. What the router does and does not do here
Sensitive nodes are NOT routed: the t / m trunks (Metal5, outside the array at both ends), the t strap to the comparator pad and the vref strap from the Cref plate to the comparator pad are drawn (Metal5 + via stacks). The router (Metal2..Metal4, 1 um grid) connects the 13 bit nets from the DAC
pads to the driver rows, the controls from the pins, vin, vcmd and the supplies; Metal2 / Metal3 never run under the DAC plates (except the channel strip between rows 3 and 4) nor under Cref / the capacitors; Metal4 keeps 1.2 um from every plate polygon.
The comparator is two MIRROR-IMAGE halves (the same routed cell, the second placed with a mirror about the axis): preamp, Cxa, latch halves and output inverters are identical by construction; the shared nets (tl, clk, clkb) and the crossing latch nets (o1 / o2) are routed in the centre column.
